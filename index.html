--- layout: home # Index page title: Welcome to my personal website! --- I am a postdoc in mathematics at Texas A&M University under the supervision of Thomas Schlumprecht, working in Banach space theory. My research is funded by an Erwin Schrödinger Fellowship from the Austrian Science Fund (FWF). Previously, I spent a year as a Postdoctoral Visitor at York University, Toronto, under the supervision of Pavlos Motakis. Before that, I was a postdoc at the Institute for Algebra of Johannes Kepler University Linz under the supervision of Manuel Kauers. I completed my doctoral studies at the Institute of Analysis, JKU Linz, under the supervision of Richard Lechner and Paul F.X. Müller. On this website you can find my publications, slides of talks, CV and some other content related to mathematics.
